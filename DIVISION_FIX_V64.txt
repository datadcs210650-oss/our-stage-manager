V64 DIVISION FIX CHECKLIST

After deploying V64:
1. Login with Admin/Super Admin.
2. Open Phân ban.
3. You should immediately see the division catalog.
4. If older members have Ban / Bộ phận values, V64 migrates them automatically.
5. Members without any division appear in the “Chưa gán phân ban” count.
6. Open a member profile/edit modal to assign one or multiple divisions.
7. Press ↻ Đồng bộ to confirm data survives a fresh Firestore reload.
8. Logout/login and verify the catalog remains.

Security notes:
- Division create/rename/delete is Admin/Super Admin only.
- Member division IDs are validated against the current catalog before write.
- Delete uses rollback if the clubState save fails.
- No new persistent cross-account realtime listeners are created.
